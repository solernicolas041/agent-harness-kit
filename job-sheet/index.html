The Job Sheet

The Harness Canvas says what an agent is. The Loop Canvas says what makes it run. Neither says what it may touch on one real account, with money that belongs to someone else. This does.

1. Click any box and type. 2. Your text saves in this browser. 3. A blank you cannot fill is written down as missing, never guessed. 4. Leave box 6 empty on day one — you haven't made the mistakes yet.
1Scope

Exactly which accounts, and what is the one thing being defended?

account IDs, in full · core objective in one line · what is NOT in this sheet

2Read rights

What may it look at without asking anyone?

the pull it must always run first · spend windows · what reading costs money or rate limit

3Write rights — every one of them gated

A read right is a habit. A write right is a decision. Name the gate for each one.

action → who approves → hard ceiling per operation · outbound messages: draft only? · a ceiling is not an approval, you need both

4Thresholds

The numbers a decision is allowed to lean on.

currency · target cost per result · target return · monthly ceiling · alert rule

5Triggers

What wakes it up on this account, and what does it load before acting?

event → the sheets, rules and registers to read first · the check for "someone already handled this"

6Guardrails — and the scars they came from

A line only lands here after something went wrong. Do not pre-fill this from imagination — you will invent the wrong walls and miss the real one.

absolute prohibitions, each with the incident behind it · date · what it cost · settled subjects nobody may reopen

?The blanks you could not fill

This box is why the sheet works. A number you do not have is written down as missing, never guessed. An invented threshold is more dangerous than an absent one, because the agent will act on it.

to confirm — need X · who can answer · what is being decided blind until they do

? a blank is a finding, not a formatting problem read rights are given · write rights are granted, one at a time

One sheet per client. Written once, read by every agent and every human who touches that account — and rewritten the day one of them gets burned.
